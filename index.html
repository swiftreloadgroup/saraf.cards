A clear reason for every message

saraf.cards delivers transactional communications on behalf of authorized business customers. If you received a message from this domain, it relates to a verification, sign-in, account, or security action connected to a service you use.

Didn't expect a message?

  • Never share a one-time code with anyone who asks for it.
  • If you didn't request it, you can safely ignore the email.
  • For account questions, contact the service provider you use.

Security

Designed for trust

Transactional email only works when people can trust it. Our delivery practices are built to keep these messages authentic, protected from abuse, and focused on the action they relate to.

  • Authenticated sending

    Messages are sent from authenticated infrastructure, so mail providers and recipients can confirm where they came from.

  • Abuse prevention

    Sending is limited to authorized business customers, and activity is reviewed to detect and stop misuse.

  • Message integrity

    Messages stay focused on their purpose — verification and account security — and are delivered over encrypted connections wherever receiving servers support it.
